# Edexcel GCSE Spanish (1SP0, 1SP1)

Added 5 November 2026 (a member's request of 2 November, "Higher, all papers"): `/question-bank/edexcel-gcse-spanish`, beside AQA's GCSE Spanish on the GCSE shelf. It is a close copy of
`edexcel-gcse-french` (same board, same paper layout), built from the same pipeline, in `scripts/question-bank/edexcel_gcse_spanish/`. Every file is Pearson's own, from qualifications.pearson.com.
Whole papers are on R2 under `edexcel/1sp0-*` and `edexcel/1sp1-*`, crops under `bank/1sp*`, recordings under `edexcel/audio/`. There is no Spanish past-paper library page to link to.

- **1,568 parts in 52 papers:** 1,413 from 1SP0 (46 papers) and 155 from 1SP1's samples (6). Every part has marks, a topic and a scheme crop; 60 multiple choices also carry their letter. All 704 Listening parts play their own question's recording. 339 questions have the examiner's comments. 299 Foundation parts pair with their Higher twin for "Show once". All 21 topics have questions (`1.3.2` Reading has 9).
- **Higher is complete for every sitting Pearson published a scheme for:** Listening, Reading and Writing, June 2018, June 2019, November 2020, June 2022 to June 2025 and the specimen; Listening and Reading for November 2021 (see the gap below). Foundation is cut by the same code and covers the same series.
- **Series:** June 2018, June 2019, November 2020, November 2021, June 2022 to June 2025 (June 2020 and 2021 weren't sat), at both tiers, plus 1SP1's Sample Assessment Materials as "2026 specimen", with the Specification filter as the French bank's. Paper 2 (Speaking) is left out, and so are the modified papers and extra-time recordings.
- **Finding the files.** Unlike 1FR0, 1SP0's exam materials are filed under their specification (`/GCSE/Spanish/2016/exam-materials/`), so `fetch_gcse_spanish.py` finds them by free-text search on the same pattern. The path's capital `Exam-materials` varies; the pattern is case-blind. Pearson's file names say what each is (`1sp0-4h-rms-20230824.pdf`), and the 1SP1 samples are `…/Spanish/2024/specification-and-sample-assessments/` (their file numbering is out of order: `3-collated-…-paper-3-higher.pdf` and `4-collated-…-paper-2-higher.pdf`).
- **The new specification.** 1SP1 was first sat in June 2026. Pearson keeps those papers and two secure mock sets for teachers, so only its sample papers are here. Its Listening is Paper 2, and its samples are tagged to 1SP0's topics.
- **Recordings: every Listening part plays its own question.** June 2022 on, Pearson's tracked zips number tracks by question (track 0 is the introduction). June 2018, June 2019, November 2020, November 2021 and the samples are one long recording, split by `split_audio_spanish.py` the way the French bank's is: each reads out the printed title in English, a short burst before a long pause, matched in order to the paper's titles (`whisper-cli`, base.en). `audio_splits.json` records the starts and what was heard, so a re-cut needs no whisper. Five starts were placed wrongly and are pinned by hand (`PINS`), each checked by transcribing the seconds around it: June 2019 Foundation's 1 and 2, November 2020 Higher's 1 and the specimen Foundation's 3 (each recording's first questions had been taken for an example's Spanish, a "Question 2, part 3", the opening instructions, or a Spanish speaker).
  **ffmpeg's seek into these MP3s lands up to 12 seconds from where it is told** (June 2019 Foundation), so slices were cut from a WAV decoded once (`exact()` in the splitter, the same in the cutter's `encode()`), not from the MP3. The French bank's untracked sittings were cut with an MP3 seek; I haven't checked whether that bank's older Listening clips are affected, and it's worth a listen to one or two.
- **Transcripts.** Pearson publishes no separate transcripts: each is bound in at the back of its Listening mark scheme, split out as `edexcel/1sp0-1h-tr-jun24.pdf`, linked from each Listening question ("tr").
- **Cutter:** `cut_gcse_spanish.py`, the French cutter on `cut_pearson.py`'s page reading and scheme tables, imported unchanged (nothing shared was edited). Its Spanish changes: the greeting and instruction words, and these fixes to Pearson's files, each a small patch over `cut_pearson` in the script:
  - **June 2019 Foundation Listening's question paper is a scan with no text layer,** read by Apple's Vision (`cut_pearson.ocr_lines`). It dropped the "7" of question 7, so every question after it was lost: patched, plus the page margins' "DO NOT WRITE IN THIS AREA" (read as tall lines that broke the crops), a capital C read for the (c), and the paper's "14©" for 14(c) in its scheme.
  - June 2025 Reading's schemes head their first column "Key idea", not "Question number", and set a short answer on its label's line ("5 (a) to work"); June 2023 Higher Reading's translation row has its "10" in the header; its paper prints no "TOTAL FOR PAPER".
  - Marks Pearson's schemes don't print where they are read: June 2018 Higher Listening 10(i) (two correct boxes, 2 marks) and June 2022 Foundation Writing 2(b) (16).
  - The 1SP1 samples' footers ("Issue 2 July 2025 © Pearson…") end a scheme row.
- **Checks in the cutter**, each run over every paper, as the French bank's: every printed line is in a crop, and only one; no crop edge cuts a line, on the paper or the scheme; every Writing scheme line is in a section; every scheme row belongs to a part; a paper's parts add up to its printed total. The two lines it still prints for the Foundation Reading sample (scheme rows "9(d)" and "9(e)") are its translation's model responses, read as rows: the translation scheme is kept whole and shown under Question 10. Crops were then read by eye on contact sheets: the whole scanned paper, Listening, Reading, Writing (paper and scheme) and specimen samples from several series, and a scan for crops with blank margins, stray footers or running-header text, of which there were none.
- **Reports** are kept per question from each report's headings, as the French bank's. A question a report doesn't mention has none.
- **Tiers:** Pearson prints Foundation Listening's last two questions as Higher's first two, and shares a few Reading questions, and `tiers.py` pairs them.
- **Gaps:** Pearson has published **no mark scheme for the November 2021 Writing papers** (4F and 4H; only the paper and its report), so those two papers are left out: a Writing part needs its scheme. Pearson published no report ("insufficient entries") for November 2020's Foundation papers or November 2021's Foundation ones. June 2026 (1SP1) and the secure mocks are teachers only.

```bash
python3 scripts/question-bank/edexcel_gcse_spanish/fetch_gcse_spanish.py
python3 scripts/question-bank/edexcel_gcse_spanish/cut_gcse_spanish.py          # first run for the titles, without recordings for the untracked sittings
python3 scripts/question-bank/edexcel_gcse_spanish/split_audio_spanish.py       # only for a new untracked sitting: keep audio_splits.json otherwise
python3 scripts/question-bank/edexcel_gcse_spanish/cut_gcse_spanish.py
python3 scripts/question-bank/edexcel_gcse_spanish/tag_gcse_spanish.py split    # add the new questions to tags_spanish.json, then:
python3 scripts/question-bank/edexcel_gcse_spanish/tag_gcse_spanish.py merge
python3 scripts/question-bank/edexcel_gcse_spanish/build_gcse_spanish.py
python3 scripts/question-bank/tiers.py edexcel-gcse-spanish
python3 scripts/question-bank/edexcel_gcse_spanish/upload_gcse_spanish.py
python3 scripts/question-bank/upload_data.py edexcel-gcse-spanish --verify
```
