# CAIE AS & A Level Computer Science (9618)

`caie/tag_caie.py split scripts/question-bank/.work/caie-cs` writes `tag/9618-topics.txt` (20 sections, 44 sub-topics,
each with its learning outcomes up to 3,000 characters) and the batches; `merge` collects them. The brief is `tag.md`'s with
the CAIE differences in `cambridge-international.md`:

- Paper 1 (9618/11, /01) and Paper 2 (/21, /02) are AS: they take only the [AS] codes (1.x-12.x). Papers 3 and 4 can take
  any. Paper 4 is practical programming: tag by what the program does (sorting and searching 19.1, stacks, queues and linked
  lists 19.1 as the syllabus has them there, objects and classes 20.1, file handling 20.2, arrays 10.2, a function or
  procedure 11.3), main topic first, at most two.
- The pseudocode in Paper 2 is tagged by its construct (data types 10.1, a loop or selection 11.2, a procedure or function
  11.3, a file 10.3, an ADT 10.4, a trace table 12.3 testing); computational thinking (abstraction, decomposition,
  flowcharts) is 9.x, structure charts and design 12.2.
- Logic gates and truth tables are 3.2 at AS and 15.2 at A Level (K-maps, sum of products, adders).
- Assembly language: 4.2 for addressing and tracing, 4.3 for shifts and bitwise operations; A Level addressing questions
  also take 20.1 (low-level programming).
- AI: 7.1 at AS (ethics), 18.1 (neural networks, machine learning, A*) at A Level.

The 198 parts were tagged on 5 November 2026 by one reader going through every part's wording and the topic list; no part
was left with a code outside the list.
